# Research decisions and current evidence

## Frozen primary

The live research primary remains a long-only, monthly, fully invested US ETF
strategy. It combines 21/63/126/252-session relative momentum (skipping the
latest five sessions), a positive 63-session momentum filter, a 200-session
trend filter and an equity exposure cap. The three frozen references are the
equal-weight Top-8, inverse-volatility Top-8 and original six-ETF baseline.

## Reproduced full-history results

The latest `run_research.py` run used the local Yahoo adjusted-close snapshot,
next-session execution, portfolio drift, and volume-aware fills for candidates
where OHLCV is enabled. Current summary:

- Equal-weight Top-8: annualized return 7.98%, Sharpe 0.72, max drawdown
  -18.35%.
- Inverse-volatility Top-8: annualized return 8.04%, Sharpe 0.77, max drawdown
  -16.69%.
- Six-ETF baseline: annualized return 10.95%, Sharpe 0.84, max drawdown
  -29.51% under the current 8 bps execution assumption.
- Combined covariance + hold-buffer candidate: annualized return 5.35%, Sharpe
  0.57, max drawdown -19.06%.

These full-history figures differ from earlier reports because the signal now
requires complete 252-session history, the target portfolio is drifted to the
signal date before turnover controls, and execution/cost accounting is
consistent. Do not compare them as if the old and new implementations used the
same assumptions.

## Walk-forward decision

The six- and twelve-month rolling reports are generated by
`walk_forward.py`. Folds roll forward monthly and overlap; they are descriptive
and not independent samples. For the twelve-month test, the combined
covariance + buffer candidate had median OOS Sharpe about 0.60, 25th percentile
about 0.04, worst-window drawdown about -19.1%, and median invested days 100%.
At 50 bps per side, its median OOS Sharpe was about 0.25. It fails the
predeclared Sharpe and cost gates, so it does not replace the simple baseline.
The six-month results show the same failure on the 25th-percentile Sharpe and
50-bps cost gates. See `walk_forward_report.md` for the most recently selected
test horizon, plus the fold and candidate CSV files.

The historical competition-window replay is only descriptive. No setting was
selected from a single historical competition window.

## Data and execution assumptions

The original `data/prices.csv` remains untouched. The new
`data/market_data/` snapshot contains Yahoo adjusted close, raw OHLCV, quality
diagnostics and SHA-256 hashes. Yahoo is a research source; final prices,
commissions and fills must be checked against IBKR.

The execution simulator uses a 2.5% bar-volume participation cap, partial
fills, pending targets, $0.005/share commission with a $1 minimum and a 0.5%
per-order cap, plus a volume-share slippage approximation. These inputs should
be calibrated from the actual account. The Lean adapter is provided, but a
full Lean run was not possible because the Lean CLI and .NET SDK are not
installed in this environment.

## Limitations

- The ETF universe is fixed and hand-selected, so it has survivorship and
  hindsight-selection risk.
- Yahoo daily bars do not reproduce intraday order-book depth or actual IBKR
  order routing; missing volume blocks fills in the conservative simulator.
- Rolling test windows overlap, so fold counts overstate independent evidence.
- The 2026-27 competition period is short; a strategy that passed historical
  gates could still fail during the event.
- Promote a more complex candidate only if it beats the frozen references on
  out-of-sample Sharpe and drawdown, passes 50-bps cost stress, maintains the
  required invested ratio, and survives IBKR/Lean execution review.
